The waste water treatment facility’s Influent & Headworks segment receives municipal flows into a screened wet well; bar screens and a vortex grit unit remove rags and sand before Flow Equalization smooths diurnal peaks via level-based pumping and mixer control. In Primary Treatment, clarifiers settle solids with interlocks tied to confirmed forward flow and weir level; scum skimming and primary sludge withdrawal follow operator-selectable sequences. Chemical Conditioning (as required) adjusts pH/alkalinity upstream of biology, with dosing permissives linked to verified influent and storage status.

The Secondary (Biological) Treatment segment is a plug-flow activated-sludge process with anoxic/aerobic zones for BNR. Blowers and modulating air valves maintain dissolved oxygen with selectors that prevent starts unless air headers are available and minimum mixing airflow is ensured. Secondary Clarification returns biomass under solids and blanket-level supervision, while Tertiary/Advanced Treatment provides filtration for turbidity/solids polishing. Disinfection (UV or chlorination with dechlorination) completes pathogen reduction; effluent is metered at the outfall. To ensure continuity at critical hydraulics, the influent lift station employs duty/standby pumps with automatic alternation and failover integrated into the level logic.

The Utilities, Chemicals & Controls segment centralizes polymer, ferric/alum, carbon source, and disinfectant systems under a plant-wide PLC/DCS with SCADA for loops, sequences, and overrides. At least one feedforward strategy is implemented: influent flow—and where available ammonia and temperature—biases aeration airflow setpoints, with a DO-PID trimming to target; similarly, flow-paced chemical dosing provides a baseline that effluent analyzers tighten. The Sludge/Biosolids train (thickening, digestion, dewatering) operates with interlocks for transfer routing, storage capacity, and odor capture to scrubbers/biofilters, completing the facility’s intended functional segmentation for subsequent control-narrative detailing.
